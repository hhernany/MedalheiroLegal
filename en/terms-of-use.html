2. Description of service

Medalheiro is a personal collection app that turns photos of physical objects — such as running medals — into interactive 3D models, organised as a private showcase. From two photos, front and back, the App generates a three-dimensional model you can rotate and zoom, to which you can attach race details, achievements, frames and games.

The App works entirely offline and stores all data locally on the user's device.

6. 3D model generation

The three-dimensional models Medalheiro generates are a visual approximation produced from two photographs, through depth estimation and image processing executed on your device. They are not metrological scans and do not represent exact measurements, relief or proportions of the real object.

Because of that:

  • Result quality depends directly on the photos: lighting, focus, framing, contrast against the background and reflections all influence the generated model.
  • Not every object produces a good result. Highly reflective, transparent, cut-out or very flat surfaces may generate imprecise models.
  • The developer does not guarantee that generation will work for any given object, nor that any specific model will be faithful to the original.

Rescanning a medal replaces the previous model, and that replacement cannot be undone.
