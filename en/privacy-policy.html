Medalheiro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Summary

Medalheiro does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Photos of your medals never leave your iPhone: the 3D models are generated entirely on the device, with nothing sent to any server. The app works without an account, without a login and without an internet connection.

1. Data collected

Medalheiro collects no personal data. The app works entirely offline and requires no login, sign-up, user account or any form of identification.

No information is sent to external servers, third-party APIs, analytics services, tracking tools or advertising networks. The app contains no networking code: it makes no internet requests.

2. Photos of your medals

Medalheiro builds 3D models from two photos of each medal — the front and the back. Those photos can come from your device's camera or from your photo library, whichever you choose.

Photos are saved on your device only and are never uploaded anywhere. The developer has no access to them.

About photos imported from your library:

  • The app reads only the images you select, through the standard iOS picker.
  • Medalheiro does not scan, index or read the rest of your photo library.
  • The selected photo is cropped, resized and stored inside the app. The original image in your library is neither modified nor deleted.

Medalheiro never writes photos to your photo library.

3. On-device image processing

To turn two photos into a 3D model, Medalheiro uses machine learning and computer vision that run entirely on your iPhone:

  • Depth estimation through a Core ML model bundled inside the app itself, executed locally by the device's hardware.
  • Background removal through Apple's Vision framework, also executed locally.

There is no cloud AI and no image is sent anywhere for remote processing. No photo is used to train any model, ours or anyone else's.

As a result, the app generates medals normally in airplane mode, with no data connection.

4. Data storage

Everything you create in Medalheiro is stored locally on your device using Apple's native SwiftData framework. That includes:

  • The front and back photos of each medal.
  • The generated 3D geometry and the pre-rendered thumbnails.
  • The race details you enter — name, date, distance, time, location, placement, race type, notes and rating.
  • Your achievements, frames, featured medals and the Snake game high score.
  • Your profile details — name, photo and the year you started running — kept in the app's preferences.

We have no access to any of this data. It belongs to you alone.

5. Permissions requested

Medalheiro requests only two permissions, both at the moment they are needed and both optional:

  • Camera — to photograph your medals. If you decline, the app still works: just import photos from your library instead.
  • Photo library — to import existing photos, including your profile picture.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings > Medalheiro. The app requests no access to location, contacts, microphone, health, notifications or any other device data.

6. No cloud, no sync

In this version, Medalheiro does not sync data between devices and does not use iCloud, CloudKit or any other remote storage service. Your collection exists only on the device where it was created.

If you back up your iPhone through iCloud or your computer, Medalheiro's data will be included in that backup, which is managed and protected by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how those backups are handled.

Should syncing be added in the future, this policy will be updated before the feature goes live.

7. Purchases and payments

Medalheiro is free and has no subscriptions or in-app purchases. No financial data — card numbers, bank details or billing information — is collected, processed or stored.

8. Children's data

Medalheiro collects no data from any user, including minors. Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, the app complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and equivalent regulations.

9. Third-party services

Medalheiro integrates no third-party services. We do not use:

  • Analytics tools (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • Crash reporting services (Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media SDKs
  • Cloud AI services

The only components outside our own code are Apple's own frameworks — SwiftData, Vision, Core ML, SceneKit and AVFoundation — all executed locally on the device.

10. Your rights

Because your data is stored on your device only, you have full control over it. You can:

  • View all of your data directly in the app.
  • Edit or delete any medal at any time. Deleting a medal also deletes its photos and its 3D model.
  • Revoke camera and photo library permissions in iOS Settings > Medalheiro.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data at once.

There is no need to request data deletion from the developer, since none of your data is in our possession.

11. Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be published on this same page with a revised update date. We recommend checking this page periodically.

If there are significant changes to how we handle data — for example, if we begin collecting any information or syncing your collection — we will notify users through the app itself before the change takes effect.
